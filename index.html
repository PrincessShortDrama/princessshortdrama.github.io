What it does

PrincessShortDrama publishes narrated, illustrated Chinese historical drama stories on YouTube. The PrincessShortDrama Uploader is a desktop tool used only by the channel's owner to upload finished episodes to the channel's own YouTube account.

For each episode it uploads the video, sets its title, description, tags, thumbnail and English subtitles, adds it to a playlist, and schedules its publishing time. It also uploads the episode's YouTube Shorts.

Who can use it

The tool is not offered to the public. It signs in only to the PrincessShortDrama channel, with the owner's permission, using Google sign-in.
